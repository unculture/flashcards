Make flashcards by talking

Flashcards is a free app for revising. Say the question, say the answer, and the card makes itself. It was built for a dyslexic student, so nobody has to type or spell.

Download for Windows Download for Mac

Free and open source. Windows 10 and 11, and Macs with Apple silicon.

Why it helps

🎙️

Speak, don't spell

Tap the microphone and talk. Your words appear as you say them, so you can see it's working.

🔒

Private by design

Speech turns into text on your own computer. Your voice is never recorded or sent anywhere.

🌱

Calm practice

No timers and no scores. Cards you know come up less often, and tricky ones come back sooner.

✨

Tidy cards, kind marking

Optional AI help removes the “ums”, fixes misheard words and checks spoken answers gently. It never changes facts.

🔊

Listen instead of reading

Tap the speaker on any card to hear it read aloud by a natural-sounding voice.

🖨️

Print real cards

Print fold-over cards or double-sided cards on A4, ready to cut out and carry around.

How it works

  1. Make a deck for a subject, such as Science or French, by saying its name.
  2. Say a question, then say its answer. The card saves itself, and you're ready for the next one.
  3. Practise ten cards at a time. Say your answer, or tap to see it, then choose “Got it” or “Not yet”.

Installing

Windows

Run the installer. Windows may say it protected your PC, because the app isn't signed yet. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

Mac

Open the download and drag Flashcards to Applications. The first time, open System Settings › Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway.

First launch

The app downloads its speech-to-text model once, about 500 MB. After that, dictation works offline.

AI help (optional)

Tidying, marking and read-aloud use OpenRouter with your own key, added in Settings. They cost a fraction of a penny per card. Without a key, everything else still works.
